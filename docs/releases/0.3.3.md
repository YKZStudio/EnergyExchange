# Energy Exchange 0.3.3

## English

- Villagers now sell every Energy Exchange item through the vanilla trade screen. Librarians offer the table/tablet, clerics offer materials, toolsmiths and weaponsmiths offer tools, and armorers offer armor.
- Trades are ordinary 26.1+ data-driven villager-trade resources. They extend existing profession/level pools and do not replace vanilla trades, so server data packs can override or remove them in the normal way.
- Prices use emeralds plus late-game vanilla materials. Infinity offers are intentionally one-use and require 64 netherite blocks plus a nether star, preventing an early-game bypass of the armory progression.
- Ship separate, exact-target JARs: `energyexchange-mc26.1.2-0.3.3.jar` (Fabric Loader 0.18.4+) and `energyexchange-mc26.2-0.3.3.jar` (Fabric Loader 0.19.3+).

## 简体中文

- 村民现在会通过原版交易界面出售全部能量交换物品：图书管理员出售转化桌/便携转化桌，牧师出售材料，工具匠和武器匠出售工具，盔甲匠出售盔甲。
- 交易使用 26.1+ 的数据包驱动村民交易资源，加入原有职业/等级交易池，不替换原版交易；服主可按原版数据包机制覆盖或移除。
- 价格使用绿宝石和后期原版材料。无尽系列交易刻意限制为每位村民一次，并要求 64 个下界合金块和 1 个下界之星，避免在游戏前期跳过装备进度。
- 分别提供严格对应版本的 JAR：`energyexchange-mc26.1.2-0.3.3.jar`（Fabric Loader 0.18.4+）和 `energyexchange-mc26.2-0.3.3.jar`（Fabric Loader 0.19.3+）。
